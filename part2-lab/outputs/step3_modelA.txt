Imagine you're a chef trying to perfect your recipe for a specific type of cake. You create a detailed blueprint with exact measurements for each 
ingredient, including the type of flour used, oven temperature, and baking time. However, when you start serving the cake to customers, you realize that 
the recipe doesn't work well for everyone. Some customers prefer a moist cake, while others like it dry. The blueprint, which was perfect for your own 
taste buds, now overfits to the specific customer's preferences, resulting in a cake that's tailored to one person's liking, but not adaptable to others.

To reduce overfitting, you can use a simpler, more general recipe that still meets the basic requirements, but is less tailored to individual preferences. 
This approach is similar to regularization techniques in machine learning, which add constraints to the model to prevent it from overfitting to the 
training data.
