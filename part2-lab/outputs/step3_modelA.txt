Imagine you're trying to paint a picture of a cat. You spend a lot of time studying the individual hairs on a cat's face, 
learning their shapes, sizes, and colors. You create a highly detailed and accurate painting of the cat's face, but when 
you show it to a friend, they tell you that it doesn't resemble a real cat.

This is like overfitting in machine learning. You're trying to fit the noise and imperfections in your training data to 
the model, resulting in a model that is extremely good at fitting the training data but fails to generalize to new, unseen data.

To reduce overfitting, you can use techniques like regularization, which is like using a filter to block out the excess details 
in your painting, allowing you to see the bigger picture.
