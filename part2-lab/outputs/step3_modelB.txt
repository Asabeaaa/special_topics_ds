Overfitting in machine learning is akin to a student memorizing every detail of a single textbook instead of learning the fundamental 
concepts that can be applied across various resources. This results in poor performance when faced with new, unseen data because 
the model is too specialized. To reduce overfitting, one can use techniques like cross-validation, regularization, or increasing 
the training dataset. For instance, instead of relying solely on one textbook, a student would benefit from a diverse library, 
allowing them to understand the universal principles and perform better in exams representing unseen data.
