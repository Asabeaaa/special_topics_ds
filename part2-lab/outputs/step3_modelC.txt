In machine learning, overfitting is like trying to draw a perfect, detailed map of your neighborhood by painting over every single street detail. 
The more you add, the more accurate it looks, but in reality, you've covered every street so much that it becomes a useless map for navigating or 
finding a new park, just like a model that captures noise as part of its pattern. To reduce overfitting, you can use techniques like 
regularization, increasing the complexity of your model (like adding more features), or by reducing the size of the data you train on.
